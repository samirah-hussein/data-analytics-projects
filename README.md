# Lego Sales Data Analysis (R)

## Overview
This project analyses a Lego sales dataset to identify which product themes generate the highest revenue. My analysis focuses on data cleaning, revenue calculation, and visualising sales trends to support business decision-making.

## Dataset
The dataset includes information on Lego products, such as:
- Price
- Quantity sold
- Theme and subtheme
- Product details

## Tools Used
- R
- tidyverse (data manipulation)
- Data visualization

## What I Did
- Imported and explored the dataset to understand its structure  
- Created a new revenue variable (price × quantity sold)  
- Aggregated total revenue by theme to identify top-performing categories  
- Filtered data to focus on the highest-performing theme  
- Created a bar chart to visualise sales distribution across subthemes  

## Key Findings
- The **Star Wars** theme generated the highest total revenue (~$4,448) 
- Sales within the Star Wars theme were uneven, with certain subthemes contributing significantly more than others  
- Results suggest that focusing on high-performing subthemes could improve future sales and marketing strategies
